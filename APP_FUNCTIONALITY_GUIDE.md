# Insight Navigator — Simple Explanation of the App

## 1. Project description

This app is like a digital control room for a financial institution. It helps people monitor money-related operations, check risks, track fraud, follow rules and regulations, and make better decisions quickly.

In simple words: this app helps the team see what is happening in the business at a glance, just like a manager looking at a dashboard in an office.

In Kinyarwanda:

Iyi porogaramu ni ikibanza cyo kureba no gucunga ibikorwa by'imari. Iyatanga amakuru yihuse ku byago, uburiganya, amategeko, ndetse no ku bintu by'ingenzi byakora kugirango abantu bashobore gufata ibyemezo byiza vuba.

---

## 2. What this app is for

The app is built to help users:

- see the current state of the business,
- notice problems early,
- understand customers and risks,
- follow rules and compliance,
- track data coming from different systems,
- generate useful reports.

Think of it as a smart dashboard for a bank or financial company.
